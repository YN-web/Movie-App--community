# Movie App – Community 🎬

## 📖 Overview
A mobile-first movie discovery and community app designed to solve the problem of fragmented movie information and lack of social interaction.  
Built with **Figma prototypes** and designed for students, young professionals, and film enthusiasts.

## 🚀 Features
- Discover trending movies by genre
- Book tickets directly through the app
- Share reviews and opinions in a community space
- Smooth onboarding and sign-in flow
- Accessible, user-friendly design

## 🛠️ Tools
- Figma (UI/UX design, prototyping)
- Adobe XD (alternative design workflows)
- Front-end stack: HTML, CSS, JavaScript (future implementation)
- Database: MySQL (planned for backend integration)

## 📂 Screens
- Splash & Onboarding
- Sign In / Sign Up
- Home & Search
- Movie Details & Booking
- Confirmation
- Community & Reviews

## 👤 User Persona
- **Aime (23)** – University student & part-time cinema blogger  
  Goals: Discover trending movies, book tickets easily, share reviews with peers.

## ⚡ How to Run
1. Open the Figma prototype link: [Movie App Prototype](https://www.figma.com/proto/b3MumyHvV6OHYaLKYiKDUU/Movie-App--Community-?node-id=3-14)  
2. Navigate through the interactive screens.  
3. Future versions will include a working front-end and backend integration.
